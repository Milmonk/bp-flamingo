# BP-FLAMINGO: Documentation Log

## Step 4 — MarianMT Translation

**Date:** 2026-03-18  
**Status:** Completed  
**Depends on:** Step 3 (EN captions from OpenFlamingo)  
**HPC Job ID:** 16516 (gpu06)

---

### 4.1 Objective

Translate the 1,000 English captions generated by OpenFlamingo into German
and French using MarianMT. This creates the "translate baseline" mode for
comparison against direct multilingual generation.

---

### 4.2 Translation Models

| Language pair | HuggingFace ID | Parameters | Load time |
|---------------|----------------|------------|-----------|
| EN → DE | Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-de | 74M | 8.4s |
| EN → FR | Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-fr | 75M | 7.5s |

Note: MarianMT models are ~34x smaller than OpenFlamingo (74M vs 2.56B),
which makes them extremely fast for inference.

---

### 4.3 Results

**Timing:**

| Translation | Samples | Total time | Avg per caption |
|-------------|---------|------------|-----------------|
| EN → DE | 1,000 | 6.8s | 0.0068s |
| EN → FR | 1,000 | 5.8s | 0.0058s |
| **Total** | **2,000** | **29.0s** (incl. model loading) | |

Comparison with Step 3: OpenFlamingo inference took 99.3 minutes for 3,000
captions. MarianMT translated 2,000 captions in 0.5 minutes — roughly
**200x faster** per caption.

**Sample Translations:**

| EN source (OpenFlamingo) | DE translation (MarianMT) | DE reference (Multi30K) |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| A man wearing a beer hat. | Ein Mann, der einen Bierhut trägt. | Ein Mann mit einem orangefarbenen Hut, der etwas anstarrt. |
| A Boston Terrier running down a grassy field. | Ein Boston Terrier, der ein Grasfeld runterläuft. | Ein Boston Terrier läuft über saftig-grünes Gras vor einem weißen Zaun. |

| EN source (OpenFlamingo) | FR translation (MarianMT) | FR reference (Multi30K) |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| A man wearing a beer hat. | Un homme portant un chapeau de bière. | Un homme avec un chapeau orange regardant quelque chose. |
| A Boston Terrier running down a grassy field. | Un Terrier de Boston qui court dans un champ herbeux. | Un terrier de Boston court sur l'herbe verdoyante devant une clôture blanche. |

**Qualitative observations:**
- Translations are grammatically correct in both DE and FR
- Translations faithfully preserve the content of the EN caption
- Differences from references stem from the EN caption itself (e.g., "beer hat"
  vs "orange hat") — the translation step does not introduce additional errors
- Compare with Direct DE from Step 3: 2/3 samples were in English, while
  translate baseline produces correct German every time

### 4.8 Design Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Translation model | MarianMT (OPUS-MT) | Lightweight, good quality for high-resource pairs |
| Batch size | 64 | Maximizes GPU utilization for small model |
| Beam search | 4 beams | Standard MT setting |
| Input | EN-only captions | Translating the best English output, not direct multilingual |
